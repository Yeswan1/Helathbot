Mental Wellness Basics

- Feeling stressed, sad, or anxious at times is common.
- Helpful coping habits can include deep breathing, journaling, and short walks.
- Talking to trusted friends, family, or a counselor can provide support.
- Limiting caffeine and maintaining sleep routines may reduce anxiety symptoms.
- If thoughts of self-harm appear, contact local emergency services or a crisis helpline immediately.

This information is supportive and educational, not a substitute for medical or psychiatric care.
