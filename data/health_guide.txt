General Healthcare Guidance

1. Hydration and Nutrition
- Drink enough water during the day.
- Include fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and protein in meals.
- Limit highly processed foods and sugary drinks.

2. Sleep and Recovery
- Adults often need around 7 to 9 hours of sleep.
- Keep a consistent sleep schedule when possible.

3. Exercise and Movement
- Regular movement can support heart health and mood.
- Even short walks can help if done consistently.

4. Preventive Care
- Routine health checkups can help detect issues earlier.
- Vaccinations and screenings should follow local medical guidance.

5. When to Seek Help
- For severe chest pain, trouble breathing, or signs of stroke, seek emergency care immediately.
- For persistent symptoms, consult a qualified medical professional.

This document is educational and does not replace professional diagnosis or treatment.
